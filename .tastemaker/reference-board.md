- https://nahiyan.codes/ (Awwwards portfolio): content set in a labelled column grid over hairlines (tldr / today / yesterday), almost no chrome.

## Borrow (DNA, not pixels)
- Type/hierarchy: Sam Goddard -> serif display carrying the whole first viewport; we use sentence case Bodoni Moda, not his all-caps didone.
- Layout/composition: Nahiyan Khan -> small mono labels above hairline rules, left label column + content column.
- Layout/composition: Deji Ajetomobi -> experience as an index/ledger rather than cards.
- Palette/material: none of the references; warm paper + ink + one vermilion "red pen" accent, chosen against the user's ban on purple/blue.
- Asset language: own signature instead of borrowed ones: a live forecast figure tied to Ammar's LTS research.

## Avoid
- Navbar + full-bleed banner photo + "What I Do?" cards (the previous site).
- Purple/blue gradients, glass cards, rounded pill buttons.
- Copying any reference's signature move (Sam's `//` status bars and dithered portrait, Deji's giant wordmark footer).

## Direction contract
- Thesis: an engineer who designs models for volatile data and ships the product around them.
- First viewport: serif statement, short bio + two actions, and Fig. 1, a live simulated forecast.
- System: paper/ink/vermilion tokens with a dark companion, Bodoni Moda + Geist + Geist Mono, hairline grid, GSAP reveals.
- Risk: the hairline Bodoni gets fragile below ~24px; keep the serif for display sizes only.
